### BASIC CONCEPTS OF A CELL

All biological systems are composed of cells containing the **same types** of chemical molecules and employing **similar principles** of organization at the cellular level.
And that's why  investigations of **model organisms** informative for understanding fundamental cellular processes.

#### What is cell

Cells are the fundamental unit of life(**The cell theory**)
- The simplest organisms are single cells
- Cells are the building blocks of tissues
- All living cells are derived from pre-existing cells

#### Prokaryotic Cell Structure and Function

- Gram Negative & Gram positive
  - Gram positive bacteria have a thick peptidoglycan layer and no outer lipid membrane 
  - Gram negative bacteria have a thin peptidoglycan, but it has outer membrane so that lysosome can't digest the peptidoglycan.

#### Eukaryotic Cell Structure and Function

![](4.png)

- **3 Types of Cytoskeletal Filaments**: Cell shape, movement, and division
  - Microtubules(20 nm): most rigid, form cilia & flagella
    - Mitotic Spindle
  
    ![](5.png)

  - Microfilament(7 nm)
  - Intermediate filament(10 nm): maintain cell tension 
- **Nucleus**
  - outer membrane connact to ER membrane
  - Nucleolus: synthesis of rRNA
  - Chromatin: DNA+ proteins(mostly histones)
- **Golgi Complex and Endoplasmic Reticulum(ER)**
  
  ![](6.png)

  - Rough ER: site of protein synthesis (lysosomal, membrane, secreted proteins)
    - protein folding
    - N-glycosylation
  - Smooth ER(tubule-like): 
    - lipid synthesis (fatty acids and phospholipids)
    - Smooth ER in the hepatocyte: principal site of production of lipoprotein particles; contain enzymes for detoxification (e.g. cytochrome 450)
    - Ca2+ storage: e.g. sarcoplasmic reticulum in the **muscle cells**
  - Golgi apparatus: each layer is different
    - cis face(close to ER,entry) and trans face(exit)
    - Receiving vesicles from rER and sER
    - Protein modifications:N- and O- glycosylation, phosphorylation
    - Packaging molecules in vesicles for transport
- **Endosomes, Phagosomes, Auto-phagosomes, Lysosomes**
  - Lysosomes: found exclusively in **animal cells**; contains acid hydrolases (nucleases, proteases, phosphatases, …) for breaking down macromolecules, organelles or autolysis of damaged cells
